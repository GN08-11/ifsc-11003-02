What is Drum Corps International?

Introduction

Drum Corps International (DCI) is a nonprofit organization that oversees competitive junior drum and bugle corps throughout North America. Founded in 1972, DCI provides a standardized system for competition, education, and performance opportunities for thousands of young musicians and performers each year. Modern drum corps combine brass performance, percussion, dance, and visual movement to create productions designed for football-field-sized venues. These performances are judged in categories including music, visual performance, and overall effect.

History of DCI

The Founding of Drum Corps International

Prior to 1972, drum corps competitions were often governed by local organizations with inconsistent rules and judging standards. Several prominent corps came together to form Drum Corps International to give participating ensembles greater control over competition structures and financial management. The organization's first championship was held in Whitewater, Wisconsin, setting the foundation for what would become one of the premier marching arts organizations in the world. Today, DCI continues to host an annual summer tour culminating in the DCI World Championships.

How Drum Corps Differs from Marching Band

While drum corps and marching bands share similarities, several key differences exist:

Membership

Drum corps performers are typically aged 16-21.
Most marching bands are associated with schools.

Instrumentation

Drum corps use only brass, percussion, and color guard.
Marching bands may include woodwinds such as flutes, clarinets, and saxophones.

Competition Season

Drum corps compete during the summer.
Marching bands typically perform during the fall.

Performance Style

Drum corps productions often focus on theatrical storytelling and advanced visual design.

Competitive Structure

Drum corps compete in World Class and Open Class divisions.
Marching bands typically compete within their school or local organization.

World Class

World Class corps represent the highest competitive level within DCI. These ensembles tour nationally throughout the summer and compete at major regional and championship events. Examples include:
  • Blue Devils
  • Bluecoats
  • Carolina Crown
  • Phantom Regiment

Open Class

Open Class corps provide opportunities for developing performers and generally operate on smaller budgets and touring schedules. These organizations emphasize education, growth, and training while maintaining high performance standards.

Life on Tour

Daily Schedule

A typical drum corps day may include:
  • 7:00 AM - Wake-up and breakfast
  • 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM - Visual rehearsal
  • 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM - Lunch
  • 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM - Music sectional rehearsals
  • 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM - Dinner
  • 6:00 PM - 10:00 PM - Full ensemble rehearsal
The rigorous schedule requires dedication, discipline, and teamwork.

Benefits of Drum Corps Participation

Participation in drum corps helps members develop: Leadership skills Time management Physical endurance Musical excellence Teamwork Personal responsibility Many alumni credit their drum corps experience with preparing them for success in college and professional careers.

Influence on Modern Marching Arts

DCI has significantly influenced contemporary marching band design. Visual concepts, drill writing techniques, percussion innovations, and performance standards pioneered by drum corps are now commonly seen in high school and collegiate marching bands across the United States. Many band directors, arrangers, and instructors are former drum corps members who bring their experience into educational music programs.

Notable Drum Corps

Blue Devils

 Based in Concord, California, the Blue Devils are among the most successful corps in DCI history, earning numerous championship titles.

Phantom Regiment

 Known for its powerful musical performances and dramatic productions, Phantom Regiment has remained a fan favorite for decades.

Carolina Crown

 Carolina Crown is recognized for innovative brass performance and strong educational programs.

Bluecoats

 The Bluecoats have gained recognition for modern artistic concepts and creative production design.
